A passport for the fediverse

You are scattered across a dozen servers. myself.social gives you one handle that vouches for all of them — and lets anyone check the claim for themselves.

How it works

  1. Claim a myself.social account and list the profiles you own — Mastodon, Pixelfed, BookWyrm, your own website, anything on the web.
  2. On each of those profiles, link back to your myself.social account.
  3. We follow every link in both directions. When two pages point at each other, the pair is confirmed and stamped.

Nobody has to take your word for it, and nobody has to take ours: the evidence is a set of public links that anyone can follow.
